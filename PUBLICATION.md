# Publication Policy

## Included
- Complete selected CTF writeups from the canonical local writeup pool.
- Self-authored challenge documentation, source, and small intended downloadable files.
- Generated indexes and provenance manifests.

## Excluded
- Raw third-party challenge files unless redistribution rights are confirmed.
- Session cookies, tokens, credentials, rejected flags, incomplete work, duplicates, and oversized files.

Historical flags are not independently re-verified by this publication step; readers should consult the original CTF terms and platform.
